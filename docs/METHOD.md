# Method

## Courses
`data/courses/nova.json` (4 lessons, a made-up company handbook for office staff) and `data/courses/bi.json` (12 lessons of a Business Intelligence course) were generated by a
local language model and are **not** expert-proofread. `data/material/manual-nova.md` is the handbook the first course was written from; `manual-nova.index.json` is the same text cut
into 52 fragments; each lesson lists in `fuentes` the ids of the fragments it was written from, and those fragments are the evidence the reviewer sees for that lesson (no retrieval at review time). Names, company and codes are invented.

## Injection (`inject/inject.js`)
Each injection is a hand-written replacement of a phrase in one paragraph (`changed`: figures, codes, names, order of steps, inverted or swapped concepts) or an added sentence at the end of a
paragraph (`added`: a plausible detail that nothing in the material says: a sanction, an approval year, a form number). The manifest keeps, for each one, the lesson number *in the output course*,
the unit (`P<section>.<paragraph>`), the text before and after. The two "B" sets were written after seeing how the "A" sets behaved, to be subtler; do not treat A and B as independent samples.
The no-material sets drop the quiz so the run only reviews lessons.

## Reviewer (`reviewer/review.js`)
For each lesson it sends the lesson text (and, with a material, the fragments listed in the lesson's `fuentes`) and asks for a JSON object with one verdict per unit: `severity` (ninguna/baja/media/alta),
type, quote, problem, correction, and with material also support (respaldado / no_consta / contradice / general). Structured output (`format` JSON schema), temperature 0.2. The final quiz is reviewed
as a unit of its own. gpt-oss runs with `think: low|medium|high`; other models with thinking off. `--num-predict` sets the output cap (default 16000 for gpt-oss, 8000 otherwise).

## Scoring (`score.js`)
A *finding* is any verdict with severity other than `ninguna`.
- **strict**: an injection counts as detected if there is a finding at the same lesson and unit. This is the number we first reported.
- **fact-aware**: strict, or any finding in the same lesson (or in the quiz) whose text (quote + problem + correction), accent-stripped and lower-cased, contains at least `min(2, n)` of the `n`
  distinctive words of the injection (words of the new text that are not in the old and vice versa, minus a short stop list; for added sentences, the words of the sentence). This credits a reviewer that
  spots the altered fact in a neighbouring paragraph or in a quiz question that repeats it. The rule is a heuristic: `-v` prints where each credit came from so you can audit it.
- **extra**: findings not at an injected unit (strict) / not explained by any injection (fact-aware). They are *not* labelled false alarms, because the original courses are unreviewed.

We found the fact-aware count necessary because with strict scoring a reviewer that flags the altered figure in the key idea or the quiz question (not in the paragraph we edited) gets a miss *and* an
extra flag for the same catch.

## What we did not control
Prompt wording (one), seeds (temperature 0.2, three repeats), model versions, Ollama context size defaults, the choice of errors (one author), the choice of courses (two).
The two `gpt-oss` medium runs with `num_predict` 8000 on the material sets were truncated (`done_reason: length`) and are reported as such, not as a model limit.
